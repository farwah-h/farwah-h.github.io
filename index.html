01 — About
Who
I am
COMSATS University
Islamabad · 2022–2026

I'm a Cybersecurity graduate of COMSATS University Islamabad, building across both the offensive and defensive sides of security. My work ranges from SOC monitoring, alert triage, and threat intelligence to low-level assembly programming, network packet forensics, and training deep neural networks against adversarial inputs.

At NECOP's Information Security Lab I performed authorised security assessments on real enterprise infrastructure — identifying misconfigurations and hardening gaps in routers and switches, running reconnaissance and service enumeration for attack surface analysis, and documenting findings in configuration-validation reports aligned with organisational security requirements.

My final year project, PhantomNet++, sits at an unusual intersection: simulating physical adversarial attacks against computer vision models, applying explainable AI to surface model decision logic and detect anomalous behaviour, and documenting findings in threat-report format to harden deep learning pipelines.

I like working where things don't usually connect — SOC operations and ML, low-level systems and high-level reasoning, offense and defense.
